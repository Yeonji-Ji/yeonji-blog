Hi, I'm Yeonji Ji.

I’m a postdoctoral researcher working where AI and chemistry meet, with a focus on f-block elements and the data foundations behind scientific discovery. I’m curious about how statistics, language models, and agentic AI can help us ask sharper questions, connect ideas, and uncover patterns that might otherwise go unnoticed.

My research roots are in computational chemistry and computer-aided drug design, and I hold a Ph.D. in Biochemistry. What keeps me excited is the space where chemistry, data, and intelligent systems come together.
